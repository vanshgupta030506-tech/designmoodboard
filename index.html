      About

      The model chooses.
      The designer defines the choices.

      You describe a feeling. Jev — a decision-only model reached through the Vercel AI Gateway — classifies your words into exactly one of four predefined directions. The interface then responds by wearing that direction’s full design system.

      Decision threshold
      70% — below it, you are shown the top two candidates and choose yourself.
      What Jev returns
      One choice plus a probability for each of the four options. Nothing else.
      What Jev never does
      Invent colours, categories, fonts or recommendations. The board is curated; only the selection is computed.
